Pebble Path - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Pebble Path is a calm side-scrolling adventure for children and their families. It works completely offline and collects no personal data.

What the game collects

Nothing. Pebble Path has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no in-app purchases and no tracking. We do not collect, receive, store on a server, share or sell any information about you or your child.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress on your own device only: the gems you found, the places you finished, the last flag you reached, and your sound, music, motion and assist settings. This file never leaves the device and is removed when you uninstall the game. It is excluded from Android cloud backup.

Network and permissions

Pebble Path does not use the internet and does not request any device permissions (no camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage access).

Third parties

The game includes no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software. It does not link to other apps or websites.

Children

Because the game collects no data at all, it collects no data from children. It contains no ads, no purchases and no social features, and no chat or sharing of any kind.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer through the contact details shown on the Pebble Path page on Google Play or the App Store.

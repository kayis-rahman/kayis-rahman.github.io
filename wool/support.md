Wool is an on-device GTD task manager for iPhone, with companion apps for Mac and Apple Watch.

## Get help

Email [apricotz.inc@gmail.com](mailto:apricotz.inc@gmail.com) with your question or feedback. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if you are reporting a problem.

## Common questions

### How does sync work?

Wool syncs through your private iCloud (CloudKit) database when you are signed in to iCloud. If sync is not working, confirm you are signed in to iCloud and have a network connection.

### Where is my data stored?

Your tasks, projects, areas, and settings are stored on your device. The developer does not run a server for task data and cannot see your private iCloud database.

### How do I export my data?

Go to Settings > Data & Privacy. Export as JSON for a full backup of tasks, projects, areas, and weekly reviews, or export as CSV for a spreadsheet of tasks.

### How do I restore a backup?

In Settings > Data & Privacy, tap "Restore from JSON" and choose your backup file. Before anything is replaced, Wool saves a JSON export of your current data to Files > On My iPhone > Wool > Pre-Restore Backups.

### How do I delete my data?

Wool does not keep your task data on a developer server. Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device. You can clear focus history and local session records in Settings.

### Voice capture or calendar is not working

Check microphone and speech recognition permissions (voice capture) or calendar permission (calendar context) in iOS Settings.
